Brooklyn is an influencer visiting Big Bend National Park. She has beauty, money to spare, and just enough notoriety to get by, but still isn't content. She feels like she's faking it.

After going to the wrong party, she wakes up deep in the desert, with no memory of what happened the night before. She runs into a gang of misfit outlaws from across the border who seem as concerned with talking about their feelings as they are with robbing.

Brooklyn realizes she prefers life in the desert to life in Los Angeles and decides to stay. As she enters a world of strange rituals, nights spent sleeping under the stars, and days riding under the hot desert sun, she wonders if the parts of her that are missing might never be found.

Blood of Agave is a modern Western novel written as a love letter to both America's rotten soul and her national parks.
